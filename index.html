01 — About me

An accountant's eye for detail, applied to data.

I'm Sean, an accounting student from Tuaran, Sabah. Accounting trained me to reconcile every figure back to its source; data analytics lets me apply that discipline at scale.

My projects work with public, verifiable data. I clean it, reconcile it against official totals, and test it using techniques auditors rely on. I frame results as areas worth a closer look, not conclusions they can't support.

Method

Reconcile first

Every dataset is checked against its source before any analysis.

Focus

Audit analytics

Tests like Benford's Law that flag what deserves review.

Mindset

Honest findings

Clear caveats and neutral wording over overclaiming.
